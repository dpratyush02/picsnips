FFmpeg.wasm

PicSnips uses the upstream browser wrapper @ffmpeg/ffmpeg 0.12.15
and the single-threaded @ffmpeg/core 0.12.10 for media processing.

The browser wrapper is under the MIT license. The stock core is
GPL-2.0-or-later and includes libraries under their respective licenses.
